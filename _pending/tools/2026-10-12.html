  • 8 October 2026 · Anthropic

    Claude makes documents, decks and designs on every plan

    Ask Claude to “turn these notes into a deck for Monday” and it builds the slides inside the chat. Docs, Slides and Design have left beta and now come with every Claude plan, Free included. Download a deck as PowerPoint or PDF, or send it to Google Slides as an editable file. You can make changes from the Claude mobile app too.

    Anthropic hasn’t said which countries get it. If you used the separate Claude Design site at claude.ai/design, it closes on 14 December 2026, and your chats and comments there won’t carry over. Save anything you need from them before then.

  • 5 October 2026 · Google

    Gemini skills arrive, and Gems will retire

    Skills are saved instructions you can call up any time you prompt Gemini, like the tone you use for customer emails. They work inside the chat, and you can stack several in one prompt. Google Workspace Business Starter, Standard and Plus accounts on the early release track get them by 12 October. Other Workspace accounts get them between 19 October and mid-November, and the Gemini app follows from 13 October.

    Google hasn’t said which countries get it. Gems keep working for now, but Google plans to stop them on business plans no sooner than 1 March 2027. Skills don’t sync between the Gemini app and Workspace, so rebuild your most-used Gem as a skill wherever you use it.
