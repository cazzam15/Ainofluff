  • 15 October 2026 · Google

    Gmail’s AI Inbox reaches paid Workspace plans, US only

    AI Inbox reads your email threads and pulls out the action items, deliverables and deadlines buried in them as suggested to-dos. It also gives you short summaries of the topics you need to catch up on. Google is adding it to Workspace Business Starter, Standard and Plus, plus Enterprise plans.

    For now it works only for users in the US with US English set as their language, so UK accounts won’t see it. Admins can’t switch it off. Google hasn’t given a UK date.

  • 14 October 2026 · Intuit

    QuickBooks AI sends invoices and builds cash flow reports

    Intuit Intelligence now reaches QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Online Advanced outside the US. Describe a recurring job in plain words and it becomes an automation that runs on its own schedule. It can create and send invoices from your customers’ own documents and chase them with reminders. Ask for a report in a chat and you get a cash flow forecast built from your books and bank activity.

    These features are in beta, and Intuit warns the AI can make mistakes. Intuit hasn’t said which countries get it or what it costs, beyond noting that extra fees may apply. Check each invoice before it goes out for the first few weeks.

  • 12 October 2026 · Google

    Google Meet drafts your follow-up email

    When a call ends, Meet’s post-meeting screen now offers next steps. Pick one and Gemini drafts a follow-up email in Gmail or adds the action items to Google Tasks. Switch a next step on once and it appears after every meeting, recurring ones included.

    It comes with Workspace Business Standard and Plus, and Google hasn’t said which countries get it. Next steps only appear if “Take notes for me” ran during the call, so turn that on before your next client meeting.
