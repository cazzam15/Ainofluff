  • 20 November 2026 · Meta

    Meta’s new business terms let it crawl your website

    Meta has rewritten the Commercial Terms that cover any business use of its products. The new version lets Meta use automated software to crawl, collect and analyse the web pages linked to your products or services. Meta says it will use that content to provide, develop, improve and research its advertising and business products.

    The new terms take effect on 20 November 2026. Keep using a Meta product for business after that date and you have accepted them; the only way to refuse is to stop. Meta’s notice doesn’t single out any country. Read the updated terms before 20 November and decide whether your shop pages and website should stay linked to your Meta accounts.

  • 22 October 2026 · Google

    Google Meet translates calls in 63 languages

    Meet’s live speech translation now covers 63 languages and spots the language each person speaks. Your supplier abroad and your client at home can join the same call, and each hears the others in their own language. Each participant switches translation on for themselves, and captions keep pace with the translated audio.

    You need Workspace Business Standard or Plus, Enterprise Standard or Plus, or a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan. Business Starter misses out. On the base plans a meeting gets up to 90 minutes of translation. Guests from outside your company can use it when the organiser has an eligible plan. Google says regional limits apply but hasn’t said which countries get it. Update the Meet app on your phone before your next overseas call.

  • 21 October 2026 · Google

    Talk a document into shape with Docs Live

    Docs Live turns the Google Docs phone app into a co-writer you talk to. Talk through a quote, a staff rota or a client proposal on the drive home, and it builds a structured document from the conversation. It can pull in context from your Gmail, Drive, Chat and the web.

    It comes with Workspace Business Standard or Plus, Enterprise Standard or Plus, and personal Google AI Pro or Ultra plans, on Android and iPhone. It switches on by default for anyone with Gemini in Docs, so admins who want it off must change their Gemini settings. Google hasn’t said which countries get it.
