  • 20 November 2026 · OpenAI

    ChatGPT Space reaches Plus: pages and slides you share with your team

    Space replaces the old Library in ChatGPT. Ask it for a page with charts, a tracker or a small interactive tool, then share it with colleagues as view or edit. Slides are arriving in Space too.

    OpenAI says it’s rolling out to Plus users aged 18 and over across the world. Anyone you share with needs a Plus, Pro, Business or Enterprise account with Space; Free users can’t open shared pages, and there are no public links. Build your weekly sales tracker there first, then share it with the one person who checks it.

  • 18 November 2026 · OpenAI

    ChatGPT gets an Accounting plugin for QuickBooks and Xero users

    The plugin helps ChatGPT reconcile transactions, draft invoice entries and put together month-end reports in your usual format. Give it a bank statement and your ledger for the same month, then ask it to list matches, possible duplicates and missing transactions. It connects to QuickBooks Online, Xero, FreshBooks and BILL, or works from Excel files you upload.

    It’s free to install, but OpenAI hasn’t said which ChatGPT plans can use it or which countries get it. You need the ChatGPT desktop app on Mac or Windows. Run last month’s bank reconciliation through it and check every flagged line against your books before approving any entry.

  • 18 November 2026 · Google

    Gemini Notebook turns your notes into a working website

    Type “make me a site” in a Gemini Notebook chat, describe the result you want, and it builds an interactive page from the sources in that notebook. Google’s examples include a quarterly sales dashboard and a project tracker for tasks and deadlines. Open it in a new tab when it’s done.

    Google says it’s available worldwide on the web for every Workspace plan and for personal Google accounts, aged 18 and over. Mobile comes later. Drop your supplier price lists into a notebook and ask for a comparison page.

  • 16 November 2026 · Google

    Share your Gemini skills with the team

    A skill you’ve built in the Gemini app, such as one that writes quotes in your house style, can now go to colleagues with view or edit access. You can also attach files from Google Drive, Gemini Notebook and Google Photos to a skill. Drive and Notebook files stay in sync, so update the price list once and the skill uses the new one.

    It’s coming to every Workspace plan and to personal Google accounts, with the rollout taking up to 15 days from 12 November. Sharing works on the web only for now, and Google hasn’t said which countries get it. Admins can switch sharing off in the Admin console.
