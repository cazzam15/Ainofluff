- Workspace, Scheduled Release domains: rollout from 19 October 2026, complete by mid-November 2026: same URL

- Gems can't be created, edited or used on business and enterprise plans "no sooner than March 1, 2027": same URL
- Skills don't sync between the Gemini app and Workspace: same URL
- No countries named in the post.
