  • 11 November 2026 · Google

    Share a Google Sheets dashboard without sharing the spreadsheet

    A Sheets canvas uses Gemini to turn your spreadsheet into a small app, such as a dashboard, a kanban board or a calendar. You can now publish that canvas to its own link. People see a read-only view and never see the sheet behind it. You choose whether the link updates when the sheet changes or stays as a snapshot.

    Google lists Workspace Business Standard and Plus, plus personal Google AI Pro and Ultra, on the web only. Google hasn’t said which countries get it. Early-release accounts get it from 11 November and everyone else from 2 December. Publish your bookings board for staff and keep the prices and notes in the sheet to yourself.

  • 10 November 2026 · Google

    Gemini in Gmail reaches every Workspace plan

    Two Gemini tools in Gmail now reach all Google Workspace customers aged 18 and over, Business Starter included. Long email threads get a summary at the top with the main points and replies. Help me write drafts an email from a short prompt or rewrites what you’ve typed.

    Both switch on by default, and each user can turn them off. Admins can turn them off for the whole account. Google hasn’t said which countries get it, and the rollout takes up to 15 days from 10 November. If customer emails hold anything sensitive, decide in the admin console whether you want it on before staff find it.

  • 10 November 2026 · Google

    Book a meeting straight from a Google Chat message

    Suggest a meeting in Google Chat and Gemini underlines the words. Hover over them and a card offers times, attendees and a title you can edit. One click books it, or you can open it in Calendar to add details.

    Google lists Workspace Business Standard and Plus. It works in English on the web, in Chat and in Gmail, and switches on by default. Google hasn’t said which countries get it, and warns it may take more than 15 days to appear from 9 November.
