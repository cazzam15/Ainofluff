## Meta’s new business terms let it crawl your website

Fetched:
- https://www.facebook.com/legal/commercial_terms_preview
- https://www.facebook.com/legal/commercial_terms

Claims:
- New terms effective 20 November 2026: commercial_terms_preview ("Effective Date: November 20, 2026") and commercial_terms notice ("These updates will go into effect on November 20, 2026").
- Meta may use automated software to access, index, cache, analyse, collect, retrieve or crawl the domains, web pages or URLs associated with your products or services: commercial_terms_preview.
- Purpose: "to provide, develop, improve, research, and maintain advertising and business products": commercial_terms_preview.
- Continued business use after notice of an update means you agree: commercial_terms_preview and commercial_terms.
- If you don't agree, you must stop business or commercial use: commercial_terms (Updates section).
- No countries named: neither page singles out a country.

Not used: news reports that the date moved from 30 October after publisher complaints (announced 23 October). Meta’s own pages show only the 20 November date, so the delay story is left out.

## Google Meet translates calls in 63 languages

Fetched:
- https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/real-time-speech-translation-in-google-Meet-expands-to-63-languages-with-automatic-language-detection.html

Claims (all from that URL):
- Published 22 October 2026; 63 languages; automatic language detection; any combination in the same meeting.
- Each participant turns translation on for themselves; captions synchronised with translated audio.
- Editions: Business Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, Google AI Pro and Ultra (plus Frontline Plus and add-ons). Business Starter not listed.
- Up to 90 minutes per meeting on eligible base Workspace and Google AI Pro plans.
- Guests from other domains can use it if the organiser has an eligible plan.
- "Availability is also subject to regional and age restrictions"; no countries named.
- Mobile users must update the Meet app.
- Rapid Release from 22 October; Scheduled Release from 2 November.

## Talk a document into shape with Docs Live

Fetched:
- https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/create-structured-documents-using-just-your-voice-with-Docs-Live-in-Google-Docs-on-mobile.html

Claims (all from that URL):
- Published 21 October 2026; voice-driven "hands-free thought partner and co-writer" in the Docs mobile app; builds structured documents from conversation.
- Draws on Gmail, Drive, Chat and web content.
- Editions: Business Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, consumer Google AI Pro and Ultra.
- Android and iOS; rolling out now.
- ON by default for users with Gemini in Docs enabled; admins manage it through Gemini settings.
- No countries named.
