  • 5 November 2026 · Google

    Gemini reads your Google Forms replies for you

    Open the Responses tab on a customer survey or booking form and Gemini spots the trends, sums up the main points and suggests next steps. It writes and runs code over your actual replies, so the counts and percentages come from your data. It also links answers across questions, such as which customers who rated you low also mentioned delivery.

    Google lists Workspace Business Standard and Plus, plus personal Google AI Pro and Ultra. Google hasn’t said which countries get it. Early-release accounts started getting it on 2 November and everyone else from 19 November, so run it on your last customer survey once it appears.

  • 5 November 2026 · Google

    Google Vids makes AI video clips up to 15 seconds

    Google Vids now runs Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, so you can generate clips from 3 to 15 seconds long. Google says the clips keep characters more consistent and handle more varied speakers. Fifteen seconds covers a product teaser or an Instagram story.

    It works on every Workspace Business plan from Starter up, and on personal Google AI Pro and Ultra. Google hasn’t said which countries get it, and it hasn’t published usage limits. Try one clip for your next offer before paying anyone to film it.

  • 4 November 2026 · Microsoft

    Free Copilot Chat in Microsoft 365 gets usage caps

    Staff without a paid Microsoft 365 Copilot licence now hit fair use limits on more Copilot Chat features. The caps started on 2 November for organisations with 2,000 or fewer Microsoft 365 seats. Microsoft won’t publish the numbers, the limits shift through the day, and admins can’t see or change them.

    Copilot Chat stays included in your Microsoft 365 subscription. Microsoft hasn’t named any countries; the change covers commercial organisations. When someone hits a cap, the message may offer to request or buy a licence, so check who can approve purchases in the Microsoft 365 admin centre before staff start clicking.
