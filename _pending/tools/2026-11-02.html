Update: 2 November 2026

  • 30 October 2026 · Google

    Gemini edits a whole Slides deck from one prompt

    Ask Gemini in the Slides side panel to restyle a deck, tighten it or pull in figures from a file in your Drive. It picks the slides to change, edits them as normal slides and lists what it did. Undo and version history still work if you dislike the result.

    It comes with Business Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, and Google AI Pro and Ultra. English only for now, and Google hasn’t said which countries get it. Next time you’d rebuild last quarter’s sales deck by hand, point Gemini at the new figures in Drive and let it do the first pass.

    Rolling out now Google’s announcement ↗

  • 29 October 2026 · Google

    Gemini builds and edits files inside the chat

    Ask Gemini for a price list, a stock tracker or a slide deck and it now shows the file in the chat. Expand it, type your changes or highlight a line and ask Gemini to rework it. Open the finished file in Slides or Sheets, download a deck as PowerPoint or PDF, or save a document to Drive.

    Free personal Google accounts get it, along with every Workspace plan. Google says it works wherever the Gemini app does, without naming countries. Spreadsheets and decks need the web version; phones handle PDFs only.

    Available now Google’s announcement ↗

  • 28 October 2026 · Google

    Sheets shows the web sources behind Gemini’s answers

    Fill with Gemini fills a column for you, such as supplier websites or opening hours for a list of shops. Each filled cell now shows the web pages Gemini used, so you can check a figure before you rely on it. One request now covers up to 3,000 cells.

    It comes with Business Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, and Google AI Pro and Ultra. Google hasn’t said which countries get it. Spot-check a handful of cited cells against their sources before you send the sheet to anyone.
