## Gmail’s AI Inbox reaches paid Workspace plans, US only

Fetched: https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/gmails-ai-inbox-is-rolling-out-to-paid-Workspace-users.html
(also https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/ for the post listing)

- Posted 15 October 2026: post date on the page above.
- Extracts action items, deliverables and deadlines from email conversations as suggested to-dos: "Automatically extracts action items, deliverables, and deadlines embedded within email conversations."
- Summaries of topics to catch up on: "Delivers clear, context-rich summaries of your most important topics."
- Editions include Business Starter, Standard and Plus and Enterprise Starter, Standard and Plus: editions list on the page.
- US only, US English: "this feature is currently only available to users in the US with EN-US as their language."
- No admin control: "There is no admin control for this feature."
- No UK date: the page gives none.

## QuickBooks AI sends invoices and builds cash flow reports

Fetched: https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1326/intuit-intelligence-now-available-internationally-fueling-success-for-businesses-and-accountants-on-one-unified-platform

- Dated 14 October 2026, datelined Toronto, London and Sydney.
- "Intuit Intelligence is now available internationally", for QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Online Advanced in international markets.
- Automations: describe a recurring task in conversation and it becomes an automation on its own schedule.
- Autonomous Invoicing: creates and sends invoices from a customer's own documents, with reminders handled automatically.
- Conversational Custom Reports: custom reports and a forward-looking cash flow view from ledger history, bank activity and predictive intelligence.
- Beta: "now rolling out, with the above features in beta."
- "Intuit Intelligence can make mistakes."
- No countries named and no prices given; disclaimer says "Additional terms, conditions and fees may apply."

## Google Meet drafts your follow-up email

Fetched: https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/find-your-routine-next-steps-on-post-meeting-screen-in-Google-Meet.html
(also https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/weekly-recap-10-16-2026.html, which links to it)

- Posted 12 October 2026: post date on the page.
- Next steps on the post-meeting screen, powered by Gemini and Workspace Studio.
- Two at launch: "draft a follow-up email" and "add action items to Google Tasks."
- Users can enable a next step across all their meetings, one-off and recurring.
- Editions: Business Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, Google AI Pro for Education.
- Rollout: extended rollout started 5 October 2026 for Rapid and Scheduled Release domains.
- Requirement: "“Take notes for me” must be enabled during the meeting for any next steps to appear."
- No countries named on the page.
