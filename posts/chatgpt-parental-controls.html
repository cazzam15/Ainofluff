How to set up ChatGPT parental controls (step by step)

Link your account to your teen's, check the defaults and set quiet hours. It takes a few minutes on a laptop.

Checked 4 November 2026 5 min read @ainofluff

Your teenager has started using ChatGPT for homework, and tonight you've sat down to sort the settings. These steps follow OpenAI's help page, which changed on 3 November 2026, so the menus may move again.

Before you start

  • You and your teen each need your own ChatGPT account.
  • ChatGPT's minimum age is 13, so these controls suit 13 to 17 year olds. For younger children, see our guide to AI and kids.
  • Use a laptop or a browser at chatgpt.com. OpenAI's invite steps are written for the web.

Step 1: Send the invite

  1. Open Settings from your profile menu.
  2. Select Parental controls.
  3. Select Add family member.
  4. Choose Use phone or Use email.
  5. Enter your teen's phone number or email address.
  6. Select Send.

Your teen can start this instead. They go to Settings, then Parental controls, then Invite a parent.

Step 2: Your teen accepts

Your teen gets a text or email with a link. If the link opens ChatGPT with no accept button, they should sign in on the web to the account that got the invite, go to Settings, then Parental controls, and accept it there.

Sent it to the wrong address? Go to Parental controls, select the person marked Invitation sent and cancel it. You can resend from the same place.

Step 3: Check the settings

Go to Settings, then Parental controls, and select your teen under Family members. Your teen can see these settings, but can't change them while your accounts are linked.

Look first at Improve the model for everyone. It starts on, so your teen's chats can help train ChatGPT until you switch it off.

Some changes only reach new conversations. Older chats may keep their previous settings.

Setting quiet hours

  1. Go to Settings, then Parental controls.
  2. Select your teen under Family members.
  3. Turn on Quiet hours.
  4. Pick a start time and an end time.

You can add more than one block, such as school nights and exam mornings. Study hours are separate and don't lock your teen out.

Limits to know about

Linking accounts gives you no access to your teen's conversations, chat history or live activity. OpenAI Support won't hand over a transcript either.

ChatGPT may notify you in limited situations, such as a serious self-harm concern. The message describes the type of concern and leaves out the conversation. OpenAI says these alerts may miss things, and some are still rolling out. They don't replace professional care or emergency services.

Turning off Health removes the feature and its synced data, but your teen can still ask general health questions.

Unlinking and turning 18

You or your teen can unlink at any time. If your teen does it, you get a notification. Your settings stop applying, and so do safety notifications.

Once ChatGPT identifies the account as belonging to someone 18 or over, parental controls end and you both get told.

Questions

Can I read my teen's ChatGPT chats?

No. Safety notifications leave out the conversation too.

Can I link more than one teen?

Yes. One parent can link several teens, but each teen links to one parent at a time.

Can I change settings by asking ChatGPT?

Some of them. Ask it to change a setting, then select Allow or Deny. Quiet hours and study hours stay in Settings.
