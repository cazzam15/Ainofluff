Free AI tools for UK small businesses

Seven free plans for writing, design, social posts, email and admin. We checked every limit on 28 October 2026.

Updated October 2026 4 min read @ainofluff

You can run most of a small firm's writing, design and admin on free plans. Each one has a ceiling: a cap on AI uses, subscribers or scheduled posts. Hit that ceiling and you've proved the tool earns its keep. Pay then, and not before.

Every limit below comes from the company's own pricing page, read on 28 October 2026. Free plans change often, and several of these firms price in US dollars, so check the page again before you upgrade.

An AI assistant

Writing and admin
Claude
Free plan · Pro from $17/month billed yearly
Chat on the web, desktop and phone. It can search the web, create files, run code and remember earlier conversations. You can also connect it to your other apps.

We use Claude every day to run ainofluff. Draft the awkward customer email in it, turn a voice note into a product description, or paste in a supplier quote and ask what you've missed.

Writing and research
Google Gemini
£0 with a Google account · Google AI Plus £4.49/month
The free plan includes Deep Research, Canvas, image generation and editing, and Gemini Live, plus 15 GB of storage across Gmail, Drive and Photos.

If your business already lives in Gmail and Google Drive, start with Gemini. Google AI Plus doubles the usage limits for £4.49 a month.

Design

Graphics
Canva Free
Free · 1.6 million+ templates
Templates for posts, flyers, menus and price lists, 5 GB of cloud storage and up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses.

Twenty AI uses won't last a busy month. Spend them on the jobs that take you longest, like removing a background or resizing one design for every platform, and do the rest by hand.

Social media scheduling

Scheduling
Publer Free
Free forever · 1 brand, 3 social accounts
Three accounts (X isn't available on the free plan), 10 scheduled posts per account at a time and 25 saved drafts. Posting straight away has no limit.

We schedule ainofluff's posts with Publer. Ten queued posts per account covers two weeks at five posts a week. On the free plan, Pinterest accounts need at least 100 followers or a claimed website before they'll connect.

Short on caption ideas? Our 5 free Instagram caption templates each come with a worked example.

Email newsletters

Email
MailerLite Free
Free · 250 subscribers, 2,500 emails a month
Includes a limited AI subject line generator. Paid plans add the AI writing assistant.
Email
Buttondown
Free for your first 100 subscribers
A rich text or Markdown editor, sending from your own domain and a hosted archive. Extras such as analytics cost $9 a month each.

We use both: MailerLite for one of our sites and Buttondown for the ainofluff newsletter. Pick MailerLite if you want designed emails, and Buttondown if you'd rather write something that reads like a letter.

Automation

Admin
Zapier Free
Free · 100 tasks a month
Connect two apps so one event triggers another: a new order adds a row to a spreadsheet, a form entry creates a task. Free Zaps run two steps and check for new data every 15 minutes.

A hundred tasks a month is enough to test one job you do by hand every week. Automate that, watch it for a month, then decide whether it's worth paying for more.

Before you start

Check each tool's settings for an option to stop it training on your data before you paste in customer details. Then pick the one job that eats most of your week, find the free tool above that handles it, and use it every day for a month.
