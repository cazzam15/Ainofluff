The AI stack a small business needs

Four layers. No tech team. Starts free.

Updated September 2026 5 min read @ainofluff

Most small businesses don't need more AI tools. They need three or four, set up properly, doing real work.

Nearly half of UK small businesses now use AI tools, up from 22% last year, according to a Simply Business survey of 1,842 firms in August 2026. Only 19% of owners feel very confident using them. Getting hold of the tools is easy. Picking the right ones is the hard part.

47% UK small businesses using AI tools in 2026, up from 22% in 2025. Simply Business, August 2026

Here's a stack for a small business running lean. We checked every price in September 2026. Several of these tools bill in US dollars, so the pound figures are approximate.

Layer 1: Your AI assistant

Layer 1
The brain
ChatGPT or Claude · free to about £20/month
Draft emails, write product descriptions, answer awkward customer messages, plan promotions and summarise research. Anything you'd otherwise stare at a blank screen for.

Start here. Both have useful free plans. If you hit the free limits, ChatGPT Go costs about £6 a month in the UK, and ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro both come to around £20. Pick one and learn it properly before you add anything else.

Layer 2: Content engine

Layer 2
The output machine
Buffer · free for 3 channels, then about £4 per channel
Write first drafts of captions and emails in your AI assistant, then schedule posts across your social accounts from one place. The drafts still need editing, but editing beats starting from nothing.

Buffer's free plan covers three social accounts with 10 scheduled posts each. Specialist writing tools like Jasper start at $59 a month billed yearly. That suits a marketing team. For most small businesses, the assistant from layer 1 handles the writing.

If your customers find you on Instagram or TikTok, I built Clutvi for this layer. It writes captions, suggests replies to comments and turns a brain dump into a month of post ideas. It costs £9.99 a month. Full disclosure: it's my product.

Short on caption ideas? Our 5 free Instagram caption templates each come with a worked example. Also ours, and free.

Layer 3: Automation

Layer 3
The connective tissue
Zapier or Make · free, then from about £7/month
Connect your tools and cut the manual steps between them. A new order sends a thank-you email. A form submission creates a task. A sale updates a spreadsheet. No code needed.

Most people skip this layer, and it's where the time savings add up. Zapier's free plan gives you 100 tasks a month, enough to test one automation. Make's Core plan costs $9 a month billed yearly, and Zapier's Pro plan starts at $19.99. Pick one job you do by hand every week, automate it, and build from there.

Layer 4: Customer service

Layer 4
The always-on layer
Tidio · free, then from about £18/month
If customers keep asking the same things (where's my order, do you ship to X, what's your returns policy), a chatbot answers them while you sleep. It learns from your website and FAQs.

Tidio's free plan includes 50 conversations with its Lyro AI agent, so you can see if it earns its place before you pay. The Starter plan costs $24.17 a month billed yearly.

Security and privacy topped owners' worries in the same survey, named by 44%. Before you paste customer details into any AI tool, check its settings for an option to stop it training on your data.

Where to start

Pick the one problem that eats most of your week: content, customer questions or admin. Find the tool in this stack that fixes it. Use it every day for a month. Then add the next layer.

That's it. No tech team required.
