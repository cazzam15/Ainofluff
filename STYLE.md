# Style: Stop Slop rules

Apply these to all ainofluff copy: posts, captions, pages and newsletters.

- Cut adverbs and filler words
- Active voice only
- No sentences starting with Who, What, When, Where, Why or Which
- No em dashes
- No "not X, it's Y" contrasts
- No vague declaratives
- No pull-quote sentences
- Vary sentence rhythm
- Trust the reader
- Put the reader in the room
- Before handing copy over, reread it for directness, rhythm, trust, authenticity and density, and revise the weakest lines.
- British English, GBP pricing.
- Fact-check every statistic against its original source before publishing.
