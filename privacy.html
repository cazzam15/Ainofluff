Privacy policy

What this site collects, why, and how to change your mind.

Last updated 1 November 2026

Who's responsible

ainofluff is run by C. Morrison in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the information below. Contact: [EMAIL].

Analytics and cookies

  • Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_…, lasting up to 2 years), so it only runs if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you click "Reject", it never loads. It records the pages you view, your device and browser, your approximate location, and a random ID kept in the cookie. It's set up for statistics only, with no advertising features. The legal basis is your consent. Google keeps this data for 14 months.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics doesn't set cookies or follow you across other sites. It only records things like which page was viewed, the site you came from, and your browser and country. The legal basis is the site's legitimate interest in knowing which pages are useful.

You can change your choice at any time: open cookie settings. Choosing "Reject" removes the Google Analytics cookies from this site. Your browser also remembers your cookie choice, which isn't personal data.

The newsletter

If you sign up, your email address is stored and the newsletter is sent by Buttondown. You'll get an email to confirm first. The legal basis is your consent, and every email has an unsubscribe link. Your address is kept until you unsubscribe or ask for it to be deleted.

The shop and other links

Products in the shop are sold through Gumroad. When you buy there, or follow a link to Instagram, YouTube or anywhere else, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Who else handles data

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and keeps standard web request logs, including your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, which reveals your IP address to Google as it does on most websites.
  • Google (Google Analytics, only if you accept) and Cloudflare process the visit statistics above.
  • Buttondown stores and sends the newsletter.

Some of these providers are in the United States. Those transfers are covered by the UK's approved safeguards (the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the UK-US data bridge where it applies). Your data is never sold.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, at any time by emailing [EMAIL]. If you're unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
