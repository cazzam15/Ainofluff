For small businesses

New AI tools worth knowing about

Launches from the apps small businesses already use, checked against each company's own announcement. Newest first. Every entry tells you whether you can get it in the UK.

Last updated 5 October 2026

Update: 5 October 2026

  • 1 October 2026 · Shopify

    Shopify Canvas: redesign your shop by chatting

    Shopify sellers can now rebuild their store's design by describing it to Sidekick, Shopify's built-in AI assistant. Canvas lays every page of your shop out side by side, and you watch each change land as you ask for it. Shopify says a fully custom store now takes about twenty minutes.

    It's an early release. Shopify says Canvas doesn't replace the existing theme editor yet and still has gaps to close. Duplicate your theme first so you have a copy to go back to.

    Rolling out now Shopify's announcement ↗

  • 29 September 2026 · Meta

    Muse for Small Business

    Meta's AI agent can now connect to your Facebook and Instagram business accounts, plus Shopify, Canva, QuickBooks, Stripe, Slack, Notion and more. You give it a goal, like finding new customers, and it works through the steps. Meta says nothing publishes, sends or spends money without your approval.

    It launched in the US and Canada only. Meta hasn't given a UK date. It's free for most uses, with paid plans for heavier use.

    Not in the UK yet Meta's announcement ↗

  • 23 September 2026 · Google

    Gemini connects to more work apps

    Google's Gemini app now works inside monday.com, Airtable, Zoho, PandaDoc, Squarespace, Webflow and Adobe, among others. Turn an app on in Gemini's settings, or type @ and its name in a chat. Then ask Gemini to manage a project, sort a database or start a web page without switching tabs.

    Google hasn't said which countries get it first. If the apps aren't in your Gemini settings yet, check again in a week or two.

    Rolling out now Google's announcement ↗

  • 17 September 2026 · OpenAI

    ChatGPT inside Microsoft Word

    ChatGPT now sits in a sidebar in Word. Draft a document from your notes, summarise a document or rewrite a paragraph you've selected, with no copying back and forth. The same add-in already works in Excel and PowerPoint.

    Install the ChatGPT add-in from Microsoft Marketplace, open it in Word and sign in. It uses your plan's normal limits.

    Free on every ChatGPT plan OpenAI's release notes ↗

  • 11 September 2026 · OpenAI

    Custom GPTs switch off on 11 December

    If your business relies on a custom GPT, such as a quote helper or an FAQ bot, it stops working on 11 December 2026. OpenAI is replacing GPTs with plugins on every ChatGPT plan. Your past conversations stay.

    If you built the GPT, open My GPTs and choose Migrate to plugin once the option appears for your account. Test the plugin with the prompts you use most. Custom actions that link your GPT to other services don't carry across, so leave time to rebuild them.
